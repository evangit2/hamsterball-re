## Full game lore dump (author-revealed 2026-09-03 — TOP SECRET)

### Premise (part 1)
- Setting is a futuristic space station built by RFA to be the new school building.
- The students all arrived, had fun together, and grew up together. That lasted three years.
- There is a war going on. Grace, as the Ultimate Diplomat, was helping with that war.
- Grace died in a tragic accident.
- Without her, negotiations broke down and the war sparked to life. The entire Earth was killed by nuclear fallout.

- NO memory wipe. All memories are true.
- The 16 are legitimately PAST versions of themselves, pulled forward into a futuristic apocalypse they have already lived through (in the original timeline).
- Our Grace is pre-death Grace — the same Grace who will later die in the tragic accident.

### Mastermind motive + paradox plan (author-revealed 2026-09-03 — TOP SECRET, keystone part 2)
- Host-Gizmo entering RFA: rough past as orphan — saw his parents murdered in front of him. Grew up very cynical.
- Met Grace: she saw him like nobody else; she was the one light in his world. (Mirrors his Ch-bafflement at "why do you trust me?")
- When Grace died (tragic accident → talks broke down → nuclear fallout killed Earth), he spiraled.
- World went to chaos. Only a select few survived by escaping to the space station school. Those survivors ARE our cast — they spent years trying to build a new life, find a way to start again.
- Then they land on time travel. Group decides: build a time machine, go back, prevent Grace's death.
- Gizmo's secret turn: he has seen enough — thinks humanity doesn't deserve to exist anymore, can't find redeeming qualities. Works behind his friends' backs.
- His real plan: destroy the universe via giant paradox — pull their past selves to the future and have them kill each other. Each past-self death = paradox tearing the universe apart at the seams. THAT is why he wants them to die (not punishment, not despair — demolition).
- Ledger-to-zero moment: SEEING the thermonuclear war that completely destroyed the Earth. Not grief alone — witnessing it.
- Knowledge split: ALL future versions know host-Gizmo hijacked the rescue plan. NONE of the past versions (the killing-game cast) know — blissfully unaware.
- Death rule: when a past version dies, their future self blips out of existence too.
- Cosmology rule (author's tree metaphor): future-going-to-past = cutting off the branch you're standing on (only you + branch go down → alternate timeline splits off). Past-brought-to-future = cutting down the ENTIRE tree (reality itself crumbles, no branch survives).
- Reveal timing: reader does NOT learn the futures exist until the end, when time travel is revealed. No future POV until then.
- Future resistance: the future selves ARE trying to stop host-Gizmo, but future Ella's Ultimate Misfortune keeps stopping them. Help is minimal, here-and-there (reads as anomalies/glitches pre-reveal). Futures are trapped in Zone 6; after every trial they can boot up a new zone to access.
- Alt vs parallel (critical distinction): Alt-Gizmo is from an ALTERNATE universe, not a parallel one. RFA doesn't exist for him. He is a completely different Gizmo, not a branch of host-Gizmo — so killing Alt-Gizmo does NOT hurt host-Gizmo (no blip link). Host-Gizmo doesn't care about him.
